**Game Design PRD: Adventure World Creator**

**Concept**
Players can choose from diverse worlds and characters to create and play their own 3D adventure, featuring platforming challenges and simple combat against enemies using various weapons.

**Core Loop**
Select a world and character, navigate platforming environments to reach objectives, engage in combat with simple weapon mechanics, and complete the level's goal.

**Win/Lose Condition**
*   **Win:** Successfully reach the designated exit portal or defeat the level's boss character.
*   **Lose:** Player character's health drops to 0.

**Controls**
*   **Movement:** WASD keys to move forward, backward, left, and right.
*   **Camera:** Mouse to look around.
*   **Jump:** Spacebar.
*   **Attack:** Left-click mouse button to swing a selected weapon.
*   **Switch Weapon:** Number keys 1-3 or mouse scroll wheel to cycle through available weapons.

**Visual Style**
Bright, colorful, and stylized cartoon 3D environments with slightly exaggerated character designs. Worlds feature distinct themes like a lush jungle, a snowy mountain, or a futuristic city, each with unique visual elements.

**Features**
1.  Players can choose from 4 distinct playable characters, each with unique idle animations.
2.  Players can select from 5 different themed worlds (e.g., Forest, Ice, Desert, Cloud, Cave) to play within.
3.  Each character starts with 3 health points, displayed as heart icons.
4.  Players can equip and use up to 3 different weapons found in the world (e.g., a basic sword, a boomerang, a bouncy ball launcher).
5.  Enemies (e.g., small slimes, walking robots) have 1-2 health points and drop a small item when defeated.
6.  Levels are designed with at least 20 distinct platforms of varying heights and sizes, requiring jumping and precise movement.
7.  A visible timer counts down from 300 seconds for each level, adding a sense of urgency.
8.  Collectibles, such as 5 golden stars scattered throughout the level, add optional objectives for scoring.

**Scene Description**
The player sees a vibrant 3D environment representing one of the chosen worlds, such as a lush jungle with large, stylized trees, floating islands, and winding paths across mossy rocks. The chosen player character is visible in the center of the screen. Various interactive platforms exist, some static, some moving horizontally or vertically at speeds up to 2 units per second. Small, simple enemy characters patrol specific routes or stand still. Scattered throughout the scene are glowing weapons that players can pick up and collectible items like golden stars. The camera follows the player character closely as they move and jump.
