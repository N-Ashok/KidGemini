## Game Design PRD: River Oar Adventure

**Concept:**
Embark on a thrilling river journey where you use an oar to steer your boat and a trusty stick to gently nudge away incoming crocodiles, earning points for every successful interaction.

**Core Loop:**
Steer the boat down the river using the oar, watch for crocodiles approaching from the shore, use the stick to push them away before they touch the boat, earn strength points for each push, and continue navigating the river.

**Win/Lose Condition:**
*   **Win:** Successfully navigate to the end of the designated river path or reach a score of 1000 strength points.
*   **Lose:** Lose all 3 boat lives by allowing 3 crocodiles to hit the boat.

**Controls:**
*   **Steering:** Hold the left mouse button and drag left/right to move the oar, directing the boat's turn.
*   **Pushing Crocodiles:** When a crocodile is within 2 boat lengths, click the right mouse button to swing the stick.

**Visual Style:**
Bright, friendly, cartoonish 3D. Water is calm and blue, shores are lush green with simple trees, and the boat has a cheerful design. Crocodiles are slightly cartoony with visible but not frightening teeth.

**Features:**
1.  Players control a small, child-friendly boat using an oar simulation.
2.  Crocodiles appear from designated spots along the riverbanks at intervals of 5-15 seconds.
3.  A successful stick push awards 50 strength points and plays a gentle "boink" sound.
4.  If a crocodile hits the boat, it bounces off with a splash, and the player loses 1 boat life.
5.  The boat has a visual indicator showing it has 3 lives remaining.
6.  The river path gradually narrows and widens, introducing minor navigation challenges.
7.  Strength points are displayed clearly on the screen in large, readable numbers.

**Scene Description:**
The player sees a 3D view from slightly behind and above their boat, looking down a winding river. The riverbanks are lined with simple green trees and occasional bushes. The water is a calm, bright blue. The boat bobs gently. Periodically, cartoonish crocodiles with wide eyes and playful grins pop their heads out from the shore and begin to swim towards the boat. If the player clicks the right mouse button in time, a visual "swish" effect appears as the stick pushes the crocodile back towards the shore. The strength score counter sits at the top-center of the screen.
