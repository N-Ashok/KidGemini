**Product Requirements Document: Kid Racer Builder**

**Concept:**
Children design and assemble their own custom 3D cars using simple drag-and-drop tools, then race them against friends in exciting multiplayer track challenges.

**Core Loop:**
Design and assemble a 3D car using pre-set parts, join a multiplayer race session, compete on a track, and unlock new parts for future car creations.

**Win/Lose Condition:**
**Win:** Be the first player to complete 3 laps on the race track.
**Lose:** Finish the race in 4th place or lower after 3 laps.

**Controls:**
Use the **W/Up Arrow** key to accelerate, **S/Down Arrow** to brake/reverse, and **A/Left Arrow** / **D/Right Arrow** to steer left and right.

**Visual Style:**
Bright, vibrant, and cartoony 3D graphics. Cars and environments are colorful and friendly, with exaggerated proportions and smooth animations.

**Features:**

*   Players can select from 10 pre-designed car chassis types (e.g., sedan, truck, sports car) as a base.
*   Players can attach 20 types of wheels and 15 types of body parts (spoilers, fenders, horns) to their car.
*   A "Paint Booth" allows players to color their car body and wheels using 20 distinct color options.
*   Races support 4 players simultaneously on the same track.
*   Players can select one of 5 pre-designed 3D race tracks.
*   Completing a race earns players 50 in-game "Gears" currency to unlock new car parts.
*   Each car has a simulated "health" of 100 HP; collisions reduce HP, and a car with 0 HP is temporarily disabled for 5 seconds.
*   The game loads a new race session within 15 seconds of all players finishing or reaching the end-of-race screen.

**Scene Description:**
The player sees their custom 3D car from a third-person perspective. The environment is a colorful, slightly exaggerated 3D race track with a bright blue sky and green hills. Other players' 3D cars, also customized, are visible on the track, moving at speeds up to 50 units per second. Obstacles like bouncy mushrooms and ramps are present on the track. The player's car steers, accelerates, and brakes smoothly based on input.
