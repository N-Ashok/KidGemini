# Helicopter City Explorer - Game PRD

## Concept
Players pilot a friendly 3D helicopter through a colorful, stylized city, completing simple tasks to help its inhabitants. The focus is on exploration and gentle interaction within a vibrant urban landscape.

## Core Loop
1.  Player pilots the helicopter using simple controls.
2.  Player flies towards a highlighted objective (e.g., a delivery point, a lost balloon).
3.  Player interacts with the objective to complete a task.
4.  Player receives a small reward (e.g., a star, points) and a new objective.

## Win/Lose Condition
*   **Win Condition**: Successfully complete 5 unique missions within the city.
*   **Lose Condition**: The helicopter's fuel gauge depletes completely (represented by a 15-minute timer).

## Controls
*   **W/Up Arrow**: Ascend
*   **S/Down Arrow**: Descend
*   **A/Left Arrow**: Strafe Left
*   **D/Right Arrow**: Strafe Right
*   **Mouse**: Look around the environment freely.
*   **Spacebar**: Interact (e.g., pick up an item, drop an item).

## Visual Style
Bright, clean, and slightly cartoonish 3D graphics. Buildings have simple shapes and clear colors, with distinct architectural styles for variety. The helicopter is rounded and friendly, with visible spinning rotor blades. Sky is a clear blue with fluffy white clouds.

## Features
*   Players can fly a helicopter that moves forward, backward, up, down, and strafes left/right.
*   The game features a 3D city with 10 unique buildings and 5 distinct street layouts.
*   A visual objective marker (e.g., a blinking star icon) guides the player to their next task.
*   Players can collect 3 types of floating items (e.g., delivery packages, lost balloons, rescue cats) by flying over them.
*   Upon collecting an item, the player must fly to a designated drop-off zone (marked by a circle on the ground) and press Spacebar to complete the delivery.
*   A simple fuel meter depletes over time, requiring players to complete objectives before it runs out.
*   Earning 5 stars, awarded for successful deliveries, unlocks a celebratory animation.

## Scene Description
The player sees a sprawling 3D city from a third-person perspective slightly above and behind their helicopter. Visible elements include colorful, blocky buildings of varying heights, wide streets with simple car models driving slowly, and patches of green park areas. The sky is a bright blue with a few static, puffy white clouds. The player's helicopter is visible in the foreground, with its main rotor spinning at a consistent speed. Occasionally, small, stylized objects like floating red balloons or blue packages will appear, moving gently in the breeze. City lights begin to glow as a simple day/night cycle progresses towards dusk.
