**Game Title:** Airport Adventure Pilot

**Concept:** Become a pilot in a 3D world where you fly realistic airplanes around a busy airport, completing missions guided by air traffic control.

**Core Loop:**
1.  Receive flight or taxi instructions from Air Traffic Control (ATC).
2.  Control the airplane's throttle, steering, and landing gear.
3.  Perform actions like taxiing, taking off, flying simple routes, and landing safely.
4.  Achieve mission objective (e.g., successfully land, reach gate).

**Win/Lose Condition:**
*   **Win:** Successfully complete 5 flight missions (e.g., 5 successful landings, 5 successful take-offs to a designated waypoint and return).
*   **Lose:** Crash the plane (e.g., exceed 30 degrees of tilt during landing, land off the runway for more than 5 seconds, hit another object), or fail to follow ATC instructions for 3 consecutive commands.

**Controls:**
*   **Throttle Slider:** Adjust engine power from 0% to 100%.
*   **Steering Wheel (On-Screen):** Move left/right to steer the aircraft on the ground or in the air.
*   **Gear Button:** Toggles landing gear up/down (cycles between states).
*   **Flaps Button:** Toggles flaps for landing (cycles between states).
*   **Brake Button:** Applies brakes while on the ground.
*   **Yaw Control (Optional):** Buttons/slider for fine steering control in air.

**Visual Style:**
Clean, stylized 3D graphics with bright, appealing colors. Aircraft models are recognizable but slightly simplified. Airport environment is detailed with clear runways, taxiways, terminal buildings, and other aircraft that move automatically. UI elements (buttons, sliders) are large, clear, and easy for children to tap.

**Features:**
1.  **ATC Voice Prompts:** Player hears clear, spoken instructions from ATC for taxi, take-off, and landing clearances.
2.  **Interactive Landing Gear:** Pressing the gear button visually retracts or deploys the airplane's landing gear.
3.  **Throttle Control:** A slider allows precise control of engine power from idle (0%) to full (100%).
4.  **Runway Approach Indicator:** A visual guide appears on the HUD when approaching the runway for landing, showing the centerline.
5.  **Speedometer:** Displays current airspeed in knots, essential for take-off and landing.
6.  **Altitude Indicator:** Displays current height above ground level in feet, with a target altitude range highlighted for approach.
7.  **Taxiway Guidance:** Glowing lines on taxiways indicate the correct path when instructed by ATC.
8.  **Multiple Aircraft:** Player can choose from 3 different airplane types, each with slightly different handling characteristics (e.g., speed, turning radius).

**Scene Description:**
The player sees a 3D view of a medium-sized airport. The immediate view is from the cockpit of their selected airplane, showing the dashboard with instruments and the runway/taxiway ahead. The airport features clear, paved runways and a network of taxiways. Terminal buildings, hangars, and a tall control tower are visible in the background. Other AI-controlled airplanes taxi, take off, and land on the airport. The sky is a bright, clear blue. Weather is always clear and sunny. The camera can optionally follow the plane from a third-person perspective.
