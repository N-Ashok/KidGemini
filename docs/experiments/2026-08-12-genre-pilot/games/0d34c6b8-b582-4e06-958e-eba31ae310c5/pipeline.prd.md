**Game Title:** Neon Mars Racers

**Concept:** Players race futuristic neon cars on a challenging Mars track, dodging rocks and hitting speed boosters to achieve victory.

**Core Loop:** Players steer their neon car around a Martian race track, avoiding rocky obstacles and hitting blue speed boosters to maintain high speeds, aiming to complete laps.

**Win/Lose Condition:**
*   **Win Condition:** Successfully complete 3 laps.
*   **Lose Condition:** Crash into large rocks 3 times.

**Controls:** Use W/Up Arrow to accelerate, S/Down Arrow to brake/reverse, and A/Left Arrow and D/Right Arrow to steer the car.

**Visual Style:** A vibrant, neon-infused futuristic aesthetic set against a dusty red Martian environment. Cars glow with bright neon colors, speed boosters pulse with electric blue light, and the sky is a dramatic gradient of purple and pink.

**Features:**
*   The player controls a neon-lit 3D car that moves at a base speed of 20 units per second.
*   The race track features a visible lap counter that increments when the player crosses the start/finish line.
*   Blue glowing speed boosters are placed along the track; driving over one temporarily increases car speed by 50% for 3 seconds.
*   Large, textured Mars rocks of varying sizes are scattered on the track as obstacles.
*   Driving into a large rock obstacle causes the car to briefly stop and reduces its speed by 10 units per second for 1 second.
*   The game environment includes a red, dusty Mars landscape with a large, glowing purple-and-pink skybox.
*   The player's car has visible neon lighting that remains constant as it drives.
