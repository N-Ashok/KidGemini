## Product Requirements Document: My Little Farm Market

**Concept**
Manage your own 3D farm and market stall, growing crops, raising animals, and processing goods to serve an endless stream of customers and earn coins for essential upgrades.

**Core Loop**
1.  **Gather/Produce:** Player harvests tomatoes, collects eggs from chickens, or processes ingredients (e.g., uses tomatoes and sugar for jam).
2.  **Stock:** Player places gathered/produced items onto market stall shelves.
3.  **Sell:** Customers arrive, select items from shelves, and pay the player with coins.
4.  **Upgrade:** Player spends earned coins at a shop to buy permanent improvements for their farm or market stall.
5.  Repeat.

**Win/Lose Condition**
This is a never-ending game. There is no win or lose condition. The player's goal is to continuously expand and improve their market operations.

**Controls**
*   **Movement:** WASD keys for player character movement (speed: 3 units/second).
*   **Interaction:** Left-click mouse button to pick up items, place items, interact with machines, and select items for sale.
*   **Camera:** Mouse movement to rotate camera, mouse wheel to zoom in/out.

**Visual Style**
Bright, friendly, and slightly cartoonish 3D graphics with clear, readable shapes and vibrant colors suitable for children. Environments feature distinct areas for farming, animal pens, processing stations, and the market stall.

**Features**
1.  Player character is visible in 3D, controllable by the player.
2.  Customers are visible in 3D, walking to the market stall and interacting with shelves.
3.  Tomatoes can be picked from tomato plants and held in the player's inventory (max 10 items).
4.  Eggs are collected from chicken coops and automatically placed into player inventory (max 10 items).
5.  A "Jelly Machine" allows players to combine 2 tomatoes and 1 sugar (sugar obtained from a separate sugarcane plot) to produce 1 jelly jar.
6.  Items placed on market shelves (e.g., 5 tomatoes) are visible and can be purchased by customers.
7.  Each successful customer transaction awards the player 5 coins.
8.  An upgrade shop allows players to spend coins; purchasing a "Faster Watering Can" increases crop growth speed by 25%.

**Scene Description**
The scene is a vibrant, open-air farm and market area. The player character is visible in the center, capable of walking. In the foreground is the player's market stall, displaying shelves that can hold up to 20 items. To the left are rows of green tomato plants and a small chicken coop where 3 chickens roam. A simple wooden "Jelly Machine" stands near the stall. In the distance, a simple fence encloses the player's farm. 3D customers appear at the edge of the scene and walk towards the market stall, with their movement speed at 2 units/second. The player can collect items like tomatoes from plants and eggs from chickens, and place them onto the stall's shelves or use them in the Jelly Machine.
