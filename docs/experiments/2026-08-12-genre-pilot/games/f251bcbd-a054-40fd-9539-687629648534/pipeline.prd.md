**Game Design PRD: Dino World Builder**

**Concept**
A playful 3D web game where children can build and explore their own prehistoric worlds populated with friendly dinosaurs and diverse flora, interacting by walking and collecting colorful fruits.

**Core Loop**
Player places decorative world elements (like trees), walks around the 3D environment to explore, finds fruit-bearing plants, and collects fruits.

**Win/Lose Condition**
*   **Win Condition:** Collect 50 unique fruits from various plants.
*   **Lose Condition:** None; the game is open-ended for continuous exploration and building.

**Controls**
*   **Movement:** WASD keys to walk forward, backward, left, right.
*   **Camera:** Mouse movement to look around.
*   **Interaction:** Left-click to select building items from a palette or pick up fruit.

**Visual Style**
Bright, stylized, low-poly 3D art with smooth, rounded edges. Dinosaurs are friendly and cartoonish. Environments feature lush greens and vibrant colors, with diverse tree assets.

**Features**
1.  Player can select and place 15 different types of 3D trees and bushes from an in-game palette.
2.  The 3D world is procedurally generated with 3 distinct biome types (grasslands, light forest, rocky areas).
3.  Player controls a chosen dinosaur avatar that can walk at a speed of 3 units per second.
4.  There are 10 types of interactive fruit-bearing plants, each yielding a unique fruit asset.
5.  Collecting 5 fruits from a plant replenishes its fruit supply after a 30-second cooldown.
6.  Players can zoom the camera in or out up to 5 meters distance from the avatar.
7.  Two non-player controlled friendly dinosaur types roam the environment at a slow pace (1 unit per second).
8.  Each player-placed tree object has a unique shadow cast.

**Scene Description**
The player sees a vibrant, colorful 3D prehistoric landscape. Rolling green hills are dotted with various stylized trees, bushes, and unique fruit-bearing plants. Friendly, low-poly dinosaur avatars walk or stand idly; the player controls one of these dinosaurs. Birds fly in simple patterns in the sky. The player's avatar can move freely across the terrain, and interacting with plants causes fruit collectibles to appear and be gathered.
