**Game Title:** My Awesome Pet Shop

**Concept:** Players build and manage a colorful 3D pet store, caring for various animated animal friends and helping them find forever homes.

**Core Loop:**
1.  **Care for Pets:** Feed, groom, or play with available pets to keep their happiness meter above 80%.
2.  **Decorate Shop:** Arrange furniture and toys to make the store environment more appealing.
3.  **Adopt Out Pets:** Match pets with arriving customers who have specific preferences (e.g., "a fluffy pet," "a pet that likes to play").
4.  **Earn Coins:** Successfully rehomed pets earn coins, which can be used to buy more food, toys, or shop decorations.

**Win/Lose Condition:**
*   **Win:** Successfully rehome 10 pets within a 15-minute game session.
*   **Lose:** If any pet's happiness meter drops below 20% for more than 60 seconds, it becomes "sad" and cannot be adopted for that session. The game ends if 3 pets become "sad."

**Controls:**
*   **Mouse Click/Tap:** Select pets, items, or UI buttons.
*   **WASD Keys / On-screen Joystick:** Move the player character (shopkeeper) around the 3D store.
*   **Drag and Drop:** Place food, toys, or decorations within designated areas.

**Visual Style:**
Bright, friendly, and cartoony 3D with rounded edges and saturated colors. Pets are designed to be visibly cute and expressive with simple, clear animations. The store environment is clean and inviting.

**Features:**
1.  Players can care for 5 distinct pet types: puppies, kittens, hamsters, parrots, and goldfish.
2.  Each pet has a visible, color-coded happiness meter that decreases over time and increases with care.
3.  Players can drag and drop 15 different toy and food items from an inventory panel to interact with pets.
4.  The shop can be decorated with 20 unique items, such as pet beds, scratching posts, and colorful wallpaper, affecting customer appeal.
5.  8 distinct customer types will visit the store, each with a simple visual preference cue.
6.  Successfully rehoming a pet triggers a short animation of the pet going with its new owner.
7.  Coins earned range from 50 to 200 per adoption, with prices for items starting at 20 coins.

**Scene Description:**
The player sees a medium-sized, brightly lit 3D pet shop interior. Display pens and tanks for various animals are visible along the walls and in the center. A counter is at the front. The player character (a simple avatar) can move freely. Pets animate within their enclosures: puppies might wag their tails and bark softly, kittens might chase a toy mouse, hamsters run on wheels, parrots perch and squawk, and goldfish swim in their tanks. Customers (simple character models) enter from a door, look around, and approach pens.
