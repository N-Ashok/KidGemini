**Product Requirements Document: Teleport Runner**

**Concept**
An endless 3D runner where players dash through vibrant, obstacle-filled environments, collecting treasures and using a special teleport ability to bypass challenges. The game is designed to be intuitive for children, with simple controls and engaging visuals.

**Core Loop**
The player character automatically runs forward. The player dodges obstacles by switching lanes, jumping, or sliding, collects coins, and can use a teleport button to instantly move ahead, aiming to achieve the highest possible score before failing.

**Win/Lose Condition**
*   **Win:** There is no explicit "win" state; the game is an endless runner focused on achieving a high score. Progression is marked by increasing speed and complexity of obstacles.
*   **Lose:** The player loses if they collide with an obstacle 3 times.

**Controls**
*   **Swipe Left/Right:** Move the character to the adjacent lane.
*   **Swipe Up:** Jump over obstacles.
*   **Swipe Down:** Slide under obstacles.
*   **Tap Teleport Button:** Instantly move the character forward 5 meters.

**Visual Style**
Bright, colorful, and stylized 3D graphics with friendly character designs and clear visual cues for obstacles and collectibles, suitable for a young audience. Environments will be varied, such as cityscapes, fantasy lands, or futuristic tracks.

**Features**
*   The player character runs forward automatically at a consistent starting speed of 5 meters per second.
*   There are 3 lanes, and lane switching takes 0.2 seconds.
*   Obstacles (e.g., stationary barriers, moving trains) appear in lanes at randomized intervals of 1 to 5 seconds.
*   Collecting a coin adds 10 points to the player's score.
*   The Teleport button, when tapped, moves the player character a fixed distance of 5 meters forward instantly.
*   The Teleport button has a cooldown of 3 seconds after use.
*   The player has 3 lives, and each collision with an obstacle costs 1 life.
*   The score increases by 1 point for every 10 meters traveled.

**Scene Description**
The player sees their character running forward on a 3-lane track stretching into the distance. The environment is a stylized 3D world (e.g., a city with colorful buildings, a magical forest). Various obstacles like trains, walls, or gaps appear in the lanes ahead, some moving and some static. Golden coins are scattered along the track. On the screen, the player's score and remaining lives are visible, along with a distinct button for the teleport ability. The character animates running, jumping, sliding, and potentially a visual effect for teleportation.
