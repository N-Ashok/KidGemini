Welcome to Paintball Fun!

Your goal is to be the first to reach 15 points by hitting other players!

Watch out for bouncy pads and destructible crates!

Keyboard Controls:

  • WASD: Move
  • Mouse: Look around / Aim
  • Spacebar: Jump
  • Left Click: Shoot Paintball
  • 1, 2, 3 or Mouse Wheel: Switch Weapons (only Paintball Gun for now!)

Touch Controls: On-screen joysticks and buttons will appear!
