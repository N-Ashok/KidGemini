## Game Design PRD: "Crafty Worlds" 3D Game Builder

**Concept:** Crafty Worlds is a browser-based 3D game builder that empowers children to design, build, and share their own multiplayer games, including action-packed shooter-style experiences. Players can assemble worlds, place interactive objects, and define game rules using intuitive drag-and-drop tools.

**Core Loop:**
1.  **Build:** Players select a 3D template or start from a blank canvas, then drag-and-drop pre-made assets (terrain, structures, characters, weapons) into their world.
2.  **Logic:** Players connect simple visual programming blocks to define gameplay rules, such as scoring, player actions, and win conditions.
3.  **Test:** Players can immediately jump into their creation in first-person or third-person mode to test mechanics and balance.
4.  **Share:** Players publish their worlds to an online gallery for friends or the community to play.
5.  **Play:** Players browse the gallery, join multiplayer sessions of games made by others, and compete or cooperate.

**Win/Lose Condition:**
For a shooter-style game created in Crafty Worlds, players typically win by achieving a target score (e.g., reaching 15 elimination points) or by being the last player or team remaining. Players lose if they are eliminated too many times (e.g., 3 lives lost) or if their opponent/team achieves the objective first. The game creator can set these specific parameters.

**Controls (In-Game Player):**
*   **Movement:** WASD keys for forward, backward, left, right movement.
*   **Look:** Mouse movement to aim and look around.
*   **Jump:** Spacebar.
*   **Shoot/Interact:** Left Mouse Button to fire weapon.
*   **Reload/Secondary Action:** Right Mouse Button (if applicable for the weapon).
*   **Switch Weapon:** Number keys (1, 2, 3) or Mouse Wheel to cycle through equipped items.

**Visual Style:**
Bright, stylized, and friendly 3D graphics with rounded edges and vibrant colors, reminiscent of popular toy blocks and playful character designs. Weapons shoot colorful, non-threatening projectiles like paintballs, water balloons, or energy darts. Environments can range from cartoonish obstacle courses to futuristic arenas or fantasy landscapes.

**Features:**
*   **Drag-and-Drop Asset Placement:** Users can intuitively place hundreds of pre-made 3D models (walls, platforms, power-ups, characters) into their game world.
*   **Visual Scripting Blocks:** A simple, icon-based system allows kids to define game logic like "On Hit: Add 1 Point" or "On Timer End: Spawn Enemy."
*   **Pre-built Game Templates:** Includes templates for "Paintball Arena," "Target Practice," and "Obstacle Course Race" to quickly jumpstart creation.
*   **Multiplayer Lobby System:** Players can easily create or join public/private multiplayer sessions for up to 8 players, supporting co-op and competitive modes.
*   **Customizable Weapons:** Creators can choose from a variety of projectile-based tools (paint guns, dart blasters, water cannons) with adjustable fire rates (3 shots per second) and projectile speeds (20 units per second).
*   **Interactive Objects:** Players can place trigger zones, moving platforms, and destructible props that respond to player actions or predefined timers.
*   **Scoreboards and Lives System:** Built-in components allow creators to easily implement visible score tracking and player life counts (e.g., 5 lives per player).
*   **User-Generated Content Gallery:** A browsable online hub where players can discover, play, and rate games made by the community.

**Scene Description:**
The screen displays a vibrant, blocky 3D environment. Players see their character model (e.g., a cartoonish robot or animal) from a first-person perspective. In front, a brightly colored arena might feature obstacles like stacked crates, ramps, and bouncy pads. Other player avatars, marked with unique colors or simple hats, move around the space. When players fire, colorful projectiles (like red paintballs or blue water splashes) streak through the air towards targets or other players, leaving temporary visual effects upon impact. A simple HUD shows the player's current score (e.g., "Score: 7"), remaining lives (e.g., "Lives: 3/5"), and a mini-map indicating teammate positions.
